Features

Everything you need to run the frontier your way

Six core modules, one lightweight Lua menu — tuned for RedM RP servers and built to stay out of your way.

👁️

Player ESP

See nearby players through walls with names, distance and health bars — perfect for keeping tabs on your RP crew or spotting troublemakers first.

🎯

Aimbot

Adjustable FOV, smoothness and target-bone selection for precise, natural-looking aim assist during RedM combat encounters.

🛡️

Godmode

Toggle invulnerability on the fly so griefing, wildlife and stray bullets can't end your roleplay session early.

🌀

Teleport

Jump to waypoints, saved locations or coordinates instantly. Save your favorite RP hubs and move across the map in a single click.

🐎

Vehicle & Mount Spawner

Spawn wagons, carriages and mounts by name or model hash. Give your character the ride your roleplay story deserves.

🕊️

Noclip

Fly freely through the world with adjustable speed and vertical control — scout locations, build scenes, or just explore the map.

How it works

Installed and running in under five minutes

No complicated dependency chains. Three steps from download to in-game menu.

1

Download the RedM RP Menu package

Grab the latest release from the download page. You'll get the compiled Lua menu files and a short readme covering the folder layout.

2

Drop the files into your RedM resources

Copy the redm-rp-menu folder into your RedM resources directory and add ensure redm-rp-menu to your server or client config.

3

Launch RedM and open the menu

Start the game, join your roleplay server, and press the default toggle key. Every module — ESP, aimbot, godmode, teleport, spawner, noclip — is one click away.
